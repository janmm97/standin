# standin

Block the motion in Blender, cast photoreal AI actors with Higgsfield through the One CLI, and voice it with local models.

Most AI video is "prompt and pray". With standin, the blocking (where people stand, how they move, the camera path) comes from a Blender stand-in animation that you can preview and repeat. The look comes from AI actors, and the voice-over is made on your own PC. It's for marketers and content creators who want short ads and clips they can direct.

Work in progress: v0.1 is being built. See [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
